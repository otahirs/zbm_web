Another edition of Sudety Cup is ahead of us and this time we return to where it all began - Lasówka. It was in these forests that the idea for the event was born, an event that each year attracts more and more people seeking genuine contact with nature and a peaceful, welcoming atmosphere. This year’s edition once again takes you deep into the heart of Sudety wilderness - from the marshy, almost flat plain around Siwa Skała, through the route-choice heavy slopes of Góra Kłobuk, to the mosaic landscape of Mostowy Potok and the valley of Dzika Orlica where water, undergrowth and terrain details will test your focus and precision. Each stage has its own character, yet all share the same goal: quality - carefully crafted maps, thought- ful course setting and a unique atmosphere. We invite you  to take part and rediscover the Sudety step by step, con- trol by control. See you in Lasówka
The deadline for all entries is 20.07.2026. Entries are to be made via Polish Orienteering Association entry system:
